Before you dive in
Why This Is Worth Learning

This isn't just a new place to click the same buttons — it replaces several old, disconnected tools with one system built around the actual job. Here's what's different:

●
Your commission is based on the real roof, not an assumption

The old Google Sheet Upgrade Cheat Sheets made large assumptions about existing materials to get to a number. The Upgrades Commissions tab prices off what's actually on the house — existing material vs. the real upgrade — so what you see is a far more accurate reflection of your actual commission, not a rough estimate.

●
You see your commission live, before you lock in a price

Drag the slider or type a price and Your Commission updates instantly — no separate calculator, no doing the math after the fact and hoping it matches what you quoted.

●
One job number ties everything together

Build your pricing once on the Commissions tab, then apply it to the Work Order with a single button — no retyping materials or costs by hand into a second document, and no version of the numbers drifting out of sync with each other.

●
It keeps working with no signal

Prep a job while you have service and the Work Order and your upgrade options are still there at the appointment, no exceptions, no "I'll fill it out when I'm back at the office."

●
Signed paperwork ready to upload to AccuLynx, no printing or scanning

Export as Flattened PDF locks in exactly what the homeowner signed, ready to upload — no driving back to the office to scan a paper form. You're also not flattening anything yourself — one click in the Work Order does it for you.

●
Customer updates happen without you having to remember them

The T1–T8 texts go out automatically at the right milestones, so nothing slips through the cracks even on your busiest weeks — and you, production, billing, and estimating are all seeing the same conversation instead of piecing it together after the fact. You also don't have to summarize the conversation or upload screenshots of texts to AccuLynx yourself — every message auto-posts to the job file for you.

●
Every upgrade you sell gets tracked automatically the moment you submit to payroll

Submitting to the Payroll Sheet from the Commissions tab is what feeds the Company Metrics dashboards — so your commissions show up in real time, and you can see exactly where you stand against your sales peers. See what's possible income-wise and get inspired!

My Sales Responsibilities

The app has a lot of tools in it, and not everything is mandatory. Here's a clear line between what you're required to do on every job, and what's there to help you but is your call.

✓ Required — Every Job
  • Enter the real existing material for every category — for anything you can't see (like what's under the shingles), give your best guesstimate, but it's always safe to go with the lowest tier; for anything visible you're unsure about, ask production ahead of time
  • Load a job before you Save it, every time — don't skip past that confirmation warning
  • Export as Flattened PDF and upload it to AccuLynx before you consider a job's paperwork done
  • Submit your final upgrade decision to the Payroll Sheet — this is what actually gets your commission recorded and paid
  • On Insurance PT jobs, reply to the customer inside the group text thread — never in a separate one-off text
  • If a homeowner texts or calls you directly instead of the group thread, loop that conversation back into the group text so everyone stays in the loop
  • Tell the homeowner about the group text during the signing appointment — that it's coming, who's in it, and that it'll come from 404-329-5117
  • Submit any known issues to k.liss@peachtreeroof.com immediately for quick resolution
○ Optional / Recommended — Up to You
  • Building all three upgrade options (Option 1/2/3) — great for working out good/better/best pricing, but not mandatory if the job only needs one
  • Exporting the PDF Cheat Sheet for your own reference
  • Prepping and using offline mode — only matters if you already know an appointment will have no signal
  • Checking the Company Metrics dashboards to track your commission and see how you stack up against your peers
  • Adding the app to your Home Screen — strongly recommended for speed and offline reliability, but the website works either way
📌
When in doubt, it's required If something affects the homeowner's signed paperwork, your commission, or what production/billing/estimating can see on a job, treat it as required. Everything else in this list is there to make your job easier, not to add extra work.

Claim Type & Job Info
1
Choose Full Deductible or DAP Only at the top

This one toggle controls your commission rate for the whole job — get it right before you build anything else.

2
Set Date Signed
3
Enter the Job Number and tap Fetch

This pulls the roof measurements (SQ, ridges, hips, valleys, rakes, eaves) straight from that job's measurement form, so you're not retyping numbers you already collected. You can still edit any of them by hand afterward if something needs adjusting.

💲
Full Deductible vs. DAP Only changes your commission rate Full Deductible pays you 100% of the margin on every upgrade (Price Charged minus Peachtree's Cost). DAP Only pays 33% of the margin. This is why Claim Type is the very first thing you set — everything below is calculated off of it.
One card per material category
Building Material Upgrades

Shingles, Ridge Cap, Starter Strip, Underlayment, Valley/Ice & Water, Pipe Boots, Warranty, and Ventilation each get their own card. The steps are the same for each one:

1
Set "Existing" to what's already on the house

This is the baseline the homeowner already has — it's what the upgrade is being priced against.

2
Tap the new material you're upgrading them to

The row shows Upgrade cost (min charge to HO) the moment you pick one — that's the absolute floor; charging exactly that amount earns you $0 commission on this item.

3
Set the price using the Margin on Upgrade slider — or just type the dollar amount

Drag the slider to set how much over cost you're charging (0% = at cost, no commission; higher = more commission), or type directly into the HO Charge box if you already know the number you and the homeowner agreed on. Both are linked — moving one updates the other.

4
Watch Min HO Charge and Your Commission update live

These sit side-by-side under the slider so you can see exactly what you're earning on that item before you lock in the price with the homeowner.

💨
Ventilation is entered a little differently Ridge Vent is entered in linear feet (the app converts to sticks needed automatically); Box Vents, Power Vents, Solar Power Vent, and Turbine are entered as a count. Existing vs. upgrade works the same way as the other categories otherwise.
🔍
Not sure what the existing material actually is? For anything you can't see with your own eyes (underlayment, ice & water, anything already buried under the shingles), give your best guesstimate as the Existing selection — but it's always safe to go with the lowest tier if you're not sure. If you're unsure about anything you can see, reach out to production for help well before your Work Order appointment — they can walk you through tricks for identifying what's actually there. Entering the real existing material, even when it takes a little extra digging, gives a far more accurate commission number than the old Google Sheet Upgrade Cheat Sheets, which made large assumptions instead of pricing off what was actually on the house.

When you're done building the options
Saving, Options & Exporting
1
Build up to three separate pricing scenarios for the same job

Adjust the sliders/materials for a different combination, then click 💾 Save Option 1, then repeat and save as Option 2 / Option 3. This is for your own use working out good/better/best pricing — present the options and pricing to the homeowner verbally, not by showing them this tab.

2
Use ⬇ Export PDF Cheat Sheet for your own reference — not required

This isn't needed to make anything in the app work. Some reps like exporting a couple of options ahead of time to have on hand while they prep for an appointment — that's the only reason to use it. If you do, add notes (color selections, special instructions, satellite measurements) first, since they print on the cheat sheet. It's internal only, never something you print or hand to the homeowner.

3
Once the homeowner decides, click 📋 Save to Work Order

This is what makes that option available to apply on the Work Order tab (Option 1/2/3 buttons — see Section 3).

4
Once the Work Order is signed and completed, fill in the final materials on the Commissions tab and click 💾 Save to Payroll Sheet

The finished, signed Work Order is what tells you which option actually got used — make sure the Commissions tab reflects that before you submit. This sends it to payroll and syncs your commission into the Company Metrics dashboards, so don't skip it even if you've already saved options earlier.

🔒
The Commissions tab and Cheat Sheet are internal only — never show a homeowner Everything here shows Peachtree's cost and your commission, so it should never be visible to or handed to a customer. If a homeowner pivots mid-appointment and asks about something you hadn't planned on, just pull up the Upgrades Commissions tab on your phone to quickly check pricing before you answer — you don't need to show them the screen to do that.

Load, Save & Applying an Upgrade Option
1
Type the Job Number, then click 📂 Load

Pulls in whatever's already on file for that job.

Click whichever Option 1 / Option 2 / Option 3 button matches what the homeowner picked (only lights up once that option's been saved to Work Order from Section 2). ⚠️

4. Using Work Order & Upgrades Offline

The app saves to your device first, then tries to sync online — so it can keep working with no signal, but only for jobs you've prepped ahead of time on that exact device.

1
While you still have signal: Load the Work Order and Save it

Do this for every job on your route — it's saved per job number, per device.

2
Build and Save your upgrade options on the Commissions tab too

Same idea — Save Option 1/2/3 while you have signal, so the Option buttons on the Work Order tab aren't greyed out later.

3
At the appointment: Load and apply Options exactly the same way

If there's no signal, Load quietly falls back to what you saved on the device in Step 1 — you won't see a difference in how you use it.

4
Don't worry about error messages while offline

Save always writes to your device first — an error just means the online sync step failed, not that your work was lost. Save again once you're back in signal so it syncs up.

🔋
Don't clear browsing data on your device Also make sure you're actually signed in (with your name/role visible) at least once before you lose signal — there's no way to sign in for the first time with no internet.

5. Group Texting Automation (T1–T8) — What You Need to Know

Every job with a Work Type of Insurance PT automatically gets a running group text conversation with the customer, starting the day the job hits Contract/Estimate Signed. You, production, billing, and estimating are all in that same thread — as the job moves through the next milestones (LGIB, roof completed, invoiced, etc.), the next message gets added automatically, so the customer never gets more than one running conversation.

●
You don't send these — the system does, based on AccuLynx status changes

There's nothing to click. As long as the job's status and dates are accurate in AccuLynx, the right message goes out at the right time.

●
Reply in the group thread, not to the customer separately

The whole point is that everyone — you included — can see what the customer has already been told, and the customer can reach the right department without having to know who to ask.

●
Every message also posts to that job's AccuLynx file automatically

Tagged [Auto-Text Sent], all in one running thread — so you can see the full customer text history on the job file even without opening the texting dashboard.

●
Only Insurance PT jobs, and only from 7/29/2026 forward

Any other Work Type is out of scope. Jobs that already passed Contract/Estimate Signed before that date aren't retroactively enrolled.

Setting Your Client Up for Success With Group Texting

The Welcome text lands the moment the job hits Contract/Estimate Signed — often within minutes of you leaving the appointment. If the homeowner has no idea it's coming, an unfamiliar number texting them "on behalf of all departments" can look like spam. A 30-second heads-up while you're signing the contract fixes that completely.

1
Tell them a group text is coming, while you're still sitting there signing

Let them know that shortly after the contract is signed, they'll get a Welcome text kicking off one ongoing group conversation for the whole project.

2
Tell them who's in it

Sales, production, billing, and estimating are all in the same thread — so no matter who they need, they can just ask in that one conversation instead of tracking down separate phone numbers.

3
Have them save 404-329-5117 in their phone as "Peachtree Texting" right then

That one detail is what turns "who is this number and why do four people have my number" into "oh, that's the Peachtree group text they told me about." Let them know the first scheduled text will include a picture with detailed contact info for each department, so they don't need to write anything else down now.

✅
This takes 30 seconds and prevents real problems Homeowners who weren't told about the group text are far more likely to ignore the Welcome text, block the number, or call the office confused — none of which happen when you set the expectation yourself, in person, before you leave.
If something doesn't go as expected
Troubleshooting
●
Home Screen icon opens a blank page or the old login screen

Delete the icon and add it again from Section 1 — this usually means it was added before you were actually signed in, or from an old bookmark/link instead of the live address.

●
Option 1/2/3 button is greyed out on the Work Order tab

That option was never saved with 📋 Save to Work Order on the Commissions tab for that job number — go back and save it, or apply whichever option did save and fill in the rest by hand.

●
Fetch on the Commissions tab doesn't pull in roof measurements

Double-check the job number matches exactly what's on the measurement form — Fetch matches by job number, so a typo or a job that hasn't had its measurement form submitted yet will come back empty. You can still enter the Roof Geometries fields by hand.

●
Total Commission looks wrong

Check Claim Type first — Full Deductible and DAP Only pay very different rates (100% vs. 33% of margin), and it's easy to leave it on whatever it defaulted to. Also check whether an Upgrades Discount is set; it reduces your commission, not the company's cost.

●
"Unsaved changes" won't go away after tapping Save

Make sure the job number in the field matches a job you've already Loaded this session — Save on a job you haven't loaded yet will prompt you to confirm before it writes anything, to avoid overwriting existing data.

●
Work Order or Upgrades won't load with no signal

That job wasn't Loaded-and-Saved on this exact device while you still had signal (see Section 4) — there's nothing local to fall back to. Keep a paper or photo backup of key job details as a last resort.

●
A customer says they never got a T1–T8 text

Confirm the job's Work Type is Insurance PT and that it reached Contract/Estimate Signed on or after 7/29/2026 — anything outside that is out of scope by design, not a bug. If it should qualify and didn't fire, flag it rather than texting the customer manually, so the root cause gets fixed.

Quick Reference Cheat Sheet
  • App icon added to your Home Screen (Safari or Chrome), so you're not typing the address every time
  • On Upgrades Commissions: set Claim Type first (Full Deductible = 100% of margin, DAP Only = 33%), then build materials, then Save to Work Order and Save to Payroll Sheet
  • On Work Order: Load before you Save, apply the matching Option button, save every now and then, export as Flattened PDF only after signatures
  • Payment Plan Authorization page is only needed if the homeowner is on a payment plan
  • Offline appointments: Load and Save every job's Work Order and Upgrade Options while you still have signal — nothing changes about how you use it once you're out there
  • T1–T8 texts are fully automatic on Insurance PT jobs — just reply inside the group thread, never separately
